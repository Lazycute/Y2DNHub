About the Ministry

More Than a
Youth Group.

We are a community of young people committed to following Jesus, growing together, serving others, and becoming everything God has called us to be.

From local youth nights to international missions, we exist to awaken purpose in the next generation — creating space where teenagers and young adults can encounter God, build real community, and step into their calling. Not a program on a calendar. A family on a mission.

More About Us

Founded on Fire

Born from a hunger to see young people encounter Jesus for themselves.

One Family, Every Nation

A community that welcomes every young person exactly as they are.

Discipleship at the Core

Real growth in Scripture, mentorship, and everyday faith.
